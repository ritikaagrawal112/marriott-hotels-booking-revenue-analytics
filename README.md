# Marriott Hotels – Booking and Revenue Analytics (Power BI)

## Project Overview

Marriott Hotels – Booking and Revenue Analytics is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze hotel bookings, revenue performance, property performance, customer behaviour, and booking trends.

The project transforms hotel booking data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards, KPIs, charts, maps, and analytical visuals to help hotel management make data-driven decisions.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze booking volume, net revenue, and cancellation rates.
- Compare revenue performance across hotel brands and properties.
- Understand customer segments, booking channels, and guest behaviour.
- Evaluate discounts, ancillary spending, and booking lead times.
- Identify factors associated with booking outcomes and guest volume.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve hotel performance.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development
- **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between relevant datasets
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, maps, tables, and KPI cards
- **Advanced Analytics** – Key Influencers and Decomposition Tree visuals

## Dashboard Pages

The dashboard consists of seven analytical sections:

| Dashboard Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Executive Overview | Summarizes bookings, net revenue, cancellation rate, and brand performance. |
| Property Performance | Analyzes property revenue, customer ratings, stay duration, and geographical performance. |
| Booking & Guest Insights | Examines customer segments, booking channels, guest volume, and revenue drivers. |
| Revenue & Booking Drivers | Evaluates gross revenue, discounts, ancillary spending, and booking value. |
| Customer & Booking Drivers | Analyzes booking outcomes and factors associated with guest volume. |
| Overall Insights | Summarizes the key findings from the analysis. |
| Recommendations | Presents data-driven actions to support business improvement. |

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks important hospitality metrics, including:

- Total Bookings
- Total Net Revenue
- Gross Revenue
- Average Booking Value
- Cancellation Rate
- Revenue per Booking
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Length of Stay
- Average Booking Lead Time
- Total Ancillary Spending
- Unique Guests and Repeat Customers

## Business Questions Explored

The project investigates questions such as:

1. Which hotel brand generates the highest net revenue?
2. Which properties and states contribute the most to revenue?
3. Which customer segments generate the highest number of bookings?
4. Which booking channels attract the most reservations?
5. How do discounts vary across hotel brands and booking channels?
6. How do booking lead times relate to revenue performance?
7. What patterns can be observed in cancellations and no-show bookings?
8. Which factors are associated with changes in guest volume?
9. How much additional revenue is generated through ancillary services?
10. What actions could help improve hotel revenue and booking performance?

## Data Preparation

The hotel booking dataset was imported into Power BI and checked for data quality and appropriate data types. Relevant tables were connected through data modeling, and measures and calculations were created for key performance indicators.

Visualizations, including cards, charts, maps, tables, slicers, a Key Influencers visual, and a Decomposition Tree, were developed to support interactive analysis across different dashboard pages.

## Key Findings

- **Top-performing brand:** JW Marriott generated approximately ₹381 million in net revenue.
- **Total bookings:** Approximately 10,000.
- **Total net revenue:** ₹561.50 million.
- **Leading customer segment:** Corporate, with 4,414 bookings.
- **Leading booking channel:** Marriott Bonvoy, with 2,537 bookings.
- **Leading state:** Maharashtra, generating approximately ₹205.38 million in net revenue.
- **Average customer rating:** 4.12 out of 5.
- **Average length of stay:** 2.61 nights.
- **Cancellation rate:** 12.82%.
- **Ancillary spending:** ₹52.27 million in additional guest spending.
